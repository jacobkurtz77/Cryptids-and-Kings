CRYPTIDS & KINGS — BIGFOOT

Collision Course v5.0: Bigfoot knockback becomes a full damage build with collisions, aimed throws, advanced Evolutions, and visible Impact feedback.
BASIC ATTACK
Basic attack details
Mighty Swipes — Bigfoot fights up close. Every third basic attack hits harder and launches enemies, making it the higher-control melee option.
Throwing Rocks — Bigfoot fights at range. He keeps more spacing and throws basic attacks from farther away, making the run safer but less controlling.
Tree Limb — 70 base damage about once per second. Each swing sweeps a wide 110° arc, reaches farther than Bigfoot’s fists, and knocks back every enemy caught in the cleave.
ABILITIES — PICK 4
Ability effects
Earth Slam — instant 360° damage around Bigfoot and heavy knockback.
Uproot — throw a tree exactly down the live green aim line. The tree pierces through enemies in a straight path.
Honey Stash — gain a timed shield for 8 seconds, temporary armor, and empower the next Camp Recovery.
Bush Ambush — Bigfoot hides for about 1 second and is invulnerable while hidden, then bursts out for AoE damage and knockback.
Wild Frenzy — for 6 seconds Bigfoot basic-attacks much faster. The red circle should stay centered on Bigfoot while the buff is active.
Boulder Wall — gain a stronger timed shield for 5 seconds and blast nearby enemies away.
Primal Rush — 10s cooldown, 20 Rage. Bigfoot gains +100% Movement Speed immediately; the bonus rapidly decays to 0 over 5 seconds. He has 15% Damage Reduction and strong knockback resistance for the full 5 seconds. His first close-range basic consumes the remaining speed and triggers Rush Impact for up to 120 flat damage based on how much speed remained. PRIMED against a ranged target at least 170px away; connecting with that target adds +40 flat Impact damage and a brief stagger.
Savage Beatdown — 10s cooldown. Bigfoot locks onto his current target and lands three heavy hits over about 1.2 seconds. The final hit interrupts enemy attacks and applies Cracked for 5 seconds, increasing damage taken by 10%.
Starting cooldown rules
Start of room — chosen non-Ultimate abilities begin with their own starting cooldown values instead of carrying over from the last room.
Bigfoot defaults — Earth Slam, Uproot, and Savage Beatdown start READY. Honey Stash starts at 2s. Bush Ambush starts at 1s. Wild Frenzy starts at 3s. Boulder Wall starts at 2s. Primal Rush starts at 1s.
Ultimate — the selected Ultimate starts each room half charged by default unless upgrades improve it.
ULTIMATE
Ultimate details
Primal Roar — 50 Rage. Deals burst damage to all enemies, applies fear, and gives Bigfoot an 8-second combat buff.
Avalanche — 60 Rage. Drops a barrage of boulders onto warning zones across the arena. Strong room coverage, but enemies can survive if they leave the danger areas.
PASSIVES — PICK 2
Passive details
Cryptid Recovery — Camp Recovery heals 10% max HP instead of 5%.
Thick Hide — Bigfoot takes 12% less damage from all incoming hits.
Heavyweight — all knockback from Bigfoot gets stronger.
Wild Instinct — when an incoming attack has 60+ raw damage before Armor, Thick Hide, shields, or other damage reduction, Bigfoot gains Instinct for 3 seconds: +30% Basic Attack Speed and +20% Movement Speed. 8-second internal cooldown. Max Health never changes the trigger.
Ancient Strength — every third basic attack deals 60% more damage.
Forest Blood — every elite kill permanently grants extra max HP.
BIGFOOT COMBAT SYSTEM
Rage & PRIMED abilities
Rage: Bigfoot starts each combat with 20/100 Rage. Basic attacks and taking meaningful hits build Rage. All abilities spend Rage. Ultimates have the highest Rage costs.
READY: the cooldown is finished and you have enough Rage to cast.
PRIMED: the battlefield is giving that ability its ideal opportunity. You can always cast unprimed, but a Primed cast gets a unique bonus. Primal Rush primes against a distant ranged target.
Prime Evolutions: Evolutions can make a Prime condition easier to reach or make the Primed payoff stronger.
